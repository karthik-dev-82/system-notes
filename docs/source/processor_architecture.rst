Beyond the Bridges
========================

Where memory and I/O actually connect inside a modern chip -- and why
"northbridge" and "southbridge," the terms most explainers still use,
describe almost nothing built in the last decade. Grounded in the
2008-era Intel motherboard model (front-side bus, northbridge,
southbridge) for context, then a floor plan for modern x86 (Intel and
AMD) and modern ARM (mobile and server), side by side.
